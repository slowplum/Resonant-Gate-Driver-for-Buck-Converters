# Resonant-Gate-Driver-for-Buck-Converters
Power electronic converters use power-MOSFETs as a switching device. As the switching frequency scales, the
efficiency of the converter is primarily limited by the MOSFET
switching losses. Hence, the design of gate driver circuit is
crucial to keep these switching losses in check while ensuring
fast turn-on and turn-off of the power MOSFET. To address this
requirement, this paper presents an on-chip resonant gate driver
with charge-recycling mechanism, implemented in a 180nm
CMOS process.
